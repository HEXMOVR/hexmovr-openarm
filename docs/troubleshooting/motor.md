# Motor Troubleshooting

### Motor does not enable

Check power, fault state, enable command and firmware state.

### Position jumps

Check encoder scaling, wrap-around, sign convention and mechanical zero.

### Current/torque appears wrong

Check unit conversion and whether the feedback field represents phase current, q-axis current, estimated torque or another quantity.
